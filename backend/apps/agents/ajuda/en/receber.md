# Receiving goods

Screen: **Receive** (`/receber`).

Every delivery starts from a supplier document: an invoice, a simplified invoice
from the cash & carry, or a delivery note. You can also receive without any
document — the paperwork can arrive later.

How it works:

1. In Receive, open "Photograph" to send the document: a PDF on the computer, a
   photo on the phone. It can have several pages.
2. The system reads the document and proposes the lines. **What it read is not
   the truth**: it is a proposal for a person to review.
3. The receipt stays in the list under the "to review" filter
   (`/receber?estado=revisao`). Open it and review line by line (see the topic
   "rever-linhas").
4. Only the "Confirm and book in" button creates stock movements.

List filters: "to review" shows what has not been booked in yet; "today" shows
what was confirmed since this establishment's business day started.

While a receipt is unconfirmed, stock is exactly what it was before the goods
arrived.
