# Team and invitations

Screen: **Settings → Team** (`/definicoes?secao=equipa`), owner only.

There are three roles in this establishment:

- **owner** — sees everything, euro figures included, and is the only one who
  invites, configures the house and sees the team's email addresses;
- **manager** — sees all of the operation and also the euro figures of purchases
  and costs;
- **staff** — sees stock, expiry dates, counts and receipts, **without euro
  figures**.

Each person has a **name in this establishment**, and that is what appears on the
movements they record. It is not their email address.

**Inviting.** The owner creates an invitation with the email, the name and the
role. It arrives as a single-use link with a deadline; whoever opens it chooses
a password and gets in. A pending invitation can be resent or revoked.

Someone who leaves is not deleted: they are deactivated. Their name stays on the
movements they recorded, which is what keeps the history readable.
