# Settings

Screen: **Settings** (`/definicoes`), with four sections in the URL
(`?secao=aparencia|estabelecimento|navegacao|equipa`).

**Appearance** is yours and follows your account: the theme (light, dark or the
system's). The other three belong to the house and apply to the whole team —
only the owner changes them.

**Establishment:**

- the **name**, which shows at the top and in the establishment picker;
- the **time the business day starts** (04:00 by default). It decides which day
  a movement, a total, a receipt or a loss belongs to: waste recorded at 01:30
  counts on the previous day. It does **not** decide expiry dates, which are the
  date printed on the packaging;
- the **VAT regime**. Under the normal regime, VAT on purchases is deductible
  and the cost is the amount before VAT; under article 53 it is not deductible
  and goes entirely into the cost.

**Navigation:** which screens the team sees in the menu, with which names and in
which order.

**Team:** who has access and who is still to join (see the topic
"equipa-e-convites").
