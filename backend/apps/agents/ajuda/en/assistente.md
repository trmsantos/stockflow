# The assistant

Screen: **Assistant** (`/assistente`), or the button in the corner of any
screen.

The assistant answers about **this** establishment, from the data on record and
with the permissions of whoever is asking: a staff member gets no euro figures,
even when they ask for them.

What it can do:

- say what is in stock, by item, store and lot;
- say what is missing, what is expiring and what was thrown away;
- show receipts, counts, recipes, productions, the team and the house's
  configuration;
- explain how the application works and take you to the screen where each thing
  is done.

What it does **not** do:

- it records nothing. It does not book goods in, does not record losses, does
  not close counts. When you ask for an action, it explains where it is done and
  gives you the button;
- it does not predict the future and does not invent numbers;
- it does not read the monthly dashboard figures in this version.

Every answer carries its sources: which queries were made and how many rows they
returned. It answers in the language of the question.
