# Monthly dashboard

Screen: **Dashboard** (`/painel`), owner only.

It shows the month's result in three tabs (`?aba=dinheiro`, `?aba=perdas`,
`?aba=pessoal`) and a period in the URL (`?period=...`, `?month=YYYY-MM`).

The dashboard month follows the **business day**: from the 1st at opening time
to the 1st of the next month at the same time. That is what makes the days add
up to the month.

Euro figures are for the owner and the manager; a staff member does not see
them.

In this version the assistant **cannot read the dashboard figures yet** — it
takes you to the screen, and the numbers are there.

The day's work — what expires, what is missing, what arrived and is waiting to
be booked in — is not here: it is on **Home** (`/`), and it is the same for
everyone.
